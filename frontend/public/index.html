How to Play

Game Rules

🗺️
Placement Phase Select exactly 20 pixels on the 10×10 grid to hide your targets. Click "Lock In" when you're satisfied.
🎯
Attack Phase Players take turns clicking pixels to guess where the opponent hid theirs. Hits light up in the opponent's color; misses are marked with a cross.
🔥
Combo Scoring Consecutive hits multiply your score:
1st hit: +1 pt  ·  2nd hit: +99 pts  ·  3rd+ hit: up to +500 pts
A miss resets your combo and costs −1 pt.
🏆
Win Conditions Be the first to hit 11 of your opponent's pixels, or reach a score of 1000 points.
👁️
Board Toggle Use "View My Pixels / View Target Pixels" during battle to switch between your hidden board and your attack board.
